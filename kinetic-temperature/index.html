What changes with T

Average kinetic energy  ⟨KE⟩
1.0× (∝ T)
rms speed  vrms
1.0× (∝ √T)
⟨KE⟩ = 3⁄2 kBT  (per molecule)
vrms = √( 3kBT / m )   ⟹   vrms ∝ √T

Double the temperature ⟹ double the average KE, but speed rises only by √2 ≈ 1.41×. That's why the molecules don't look twice as fast.
