MAE 2030 · Anderson §5.13

Finite Wings: Tip Vortices, Downwash & Induced Drag

Front view · trailing vortices (qualitative)

Looking upstream at the wing. Flow leaks from the high-pressure lower surface around each tip to the low-pressure upper surface, rolling up into two counter-rotating vortices. Between the tips they induce a downward velocity: the downwash w. Vortex strength scales with w/V∞ = CL/(π AR).
